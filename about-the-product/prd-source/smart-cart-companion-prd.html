TL;DR Grocery shoppers plan across separate tools: weekly flyers, a budgeting app, and their own hunt for savings. The result is overspending, missed flyer savings and decision fatigue. Walmart is excellent at fulfilling orders, but it doesn't help households plan. Smart Cart Companion is an AI grocery planner inside the Walmart experience. It turns this week's flyer and rollback deals into a personalized basket, keeps that basket within the household's real budget (typed in, or pulled from YNAB), and hands it off to the Walmart cart. Groceries are bought every week, so the product is measured on habit: the North Star is Weekly Repeat Grocery Planning Usage, with a target of +25% in the first six months of a Walmart+ beta. The business payoff is Walmart+ retention. The aim is to move Walmart from a place where groceries are bought to the place where households plan them.

Section 3

Architecture

How the MVP works end to end. The shopper's inputs and budget enter on the left, a budget-checked basket comes out on the right, and the spend flows back into YNAB after checkout.

What to notice: the AI can only pick items that exist in the catalog, and every pick is validated before it reaches the cart. If the AI call fails or no key is set, the local engine builds the basket instead.

Data sources

A mock Walmart catalog of grocery products with prices, rollback deals and ratings stands in for live data. Flyer ingestion is designed so the mock catalog can be swapped for Walmart's product and flyer APIs later without changing the screens.

Basket generation

OpenAI GPT-4o-mini receives the shopper's preferences and a compact copy of the catalog, and returns a basket as structured JSON. Its rules: pick only from the catalog using exact item IDs, stay within the total budget, respect dietary restrictions strictly, prefer items on sale, and cover the major food categories. Every returned ID is checked against the catalog. When no OpenAI key is set, or the call fails, the built-in local engine ranks the catalog against the shopper's preferences with five strategies: lowest cost, healthiest, bulk saver, family and brand.

Section 4

Failure Modes & How They're Handled

Each entry starts from an assumption the product depends on and covers what breaks if it fails, how we'd notice and how we'd respond.

1Users don't trust AI recommendationsImpact: HighLikelihood: Medium

Breaks: shoppers ignore the AI basket, so basket adoption stays low.

Detect: basket conversion declines.

Fix: hybrid controls with manual overrides, so shoppers can toggle, swap and switch strategies. Contain it by adding transparency and control.

2Flyer data goes stale or wrongImpact: HighLikelihood: Medium

Breaks: the planner recommends savings that don't exist at the shelf.

Detect: a rising price-mismatch rate.

Fix: SLA monitoring on flyer freshness (target: refreshed within 24 hours), with fallback to a default optimized catalog.

3Budget sync is unreliableImpact: MediumLikelihood: Medium

Breaks: OAuth or YNAB API failures leave the planner without a budget.

Detect: the OAuth failure rate.

Fix: a manual budget fallback, so planning continues in manual mode without blocking the shopper.

4Walmart APIs aren't accessibleImpact: HighLikelihood: High

Breaks: limited real-time integration with Walmart's product, flyer and cart systems.

Detect: integration blockers during the beta.

Fix: mock data behind a scalable architecture, plus a phased rollout that works within those limits.

5AI recommendations are poorImpact: HighLikelihood: Medium

Breaks: weak personalization; baskets don't match the household.

Detect: user trust declines.

Fix: feedback loops and confidence scoring, with recommendation retraining.

6The AI service is unavailable

Breaks: the OpenAI call fails or no key is configured.

Detect: each basket is flagged as built by the AI or by the local engine.

Fix: automatic fallback to the local engine, so a basket is always generated.

Trust and privacy

  • Transparent savings breakdown: rollback savings are shown per item and in the order total.
  • Budget warnings: the basket shows when the selection goes over budget, and by how much.
  • Privacy-first integrations: YNAB and Plaid token exchanges run on the backend, so access tokens never reach the browser. In the MVP the OpenAI key is called from the browser to keep things simple; it moves to a server-side proxy before production.

Section 7

AI Build Brief

The specification the basket planner is built against, taken from the MVP's planner.

Inputs

Planning period (weekly, bi-weekly or monthly) · budget (manual or from YNAB) · household (adults, seniors, kids) · dietary preferences · pantry items · the product catalog with prices, deals and ratings.

Rules the model must follow

  1. Select only items that exist in the provided catalog, using exact item IDs.
  2. Stay within the total budget for the planning period.
  3. Respect dietary restrictions strictly.
  4. Prefer items on sale when possible.
  5. Cover the major food categories (produce, dairy, protein, pantry and others).
  6. Return only valid JSON with a strategy, a short personalized explanation and the selected item IDs.

Requirements

Mapped from the RICE prioritization tiers in Section 2.

PriorityRequirement
MUSTAI Planner: generate a personalized grocery basket from the shopper's inputs
MUSTBudget Sync: plan within the real budget, from YNAB or entered manually, and sync spend back
MUSTFlyer Automation: build baskets from this week's flyer and rollback deals
SHOULDSmart Repeat: make next week's plan faster for returning households
SHOULDWellness optimization: encourage healthier choices within budget
COULDBehavioral insights on spending and planning habits
COULDPantry intelligence: account for what the household already has

Guardrails

  • Item IDs returned by the model are checked against the catalog; unknown IDs are dropped.
  • If the model call fails or no key is set, the local engine builds the basket, so the shopper always gets one.
  • The shopper always has the final say: items can be toggled, and strategies switched, before anything reaches the cart.
